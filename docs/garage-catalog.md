# Local vehicle catalog
An original, manually assembled non-exhaustive selection of common manufacturer and model names, written for this task from general knowledge. No external database, API, scraping, copied proprietary dataset, logos, or images are used by the catalog. Names are plain factual identifiers; trademarks remain with their respective owners; no endorsement or rights clearance is implied. The list is distributed as project source, subject to the repository's applicable licensing terms. Coverage and spelling are not guaranteed. Manual brand/model entry is always supported; a typed brand filters suggestions by case-insensitive exact match. All lookup is local and no vehicle data leaves the device.
